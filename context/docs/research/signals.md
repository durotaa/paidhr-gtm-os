# PaidHR: Buying Signals

Raw research, not conclusions. All sources checked 2026-11-09.
Each signal shows its public evidence. The "why it matters" lines are `INFERRED` unless a source is quoted. Not yet validated against real deals.

## Compliance deadlines and enforcement

| Signal | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| New PAYE bands and reliefs live since 1 Jan 2026 | Employers must update payroll for bands, rent relief, gratuity, bonuses | https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/nigeria/insights/2026/FAQs-on-payroll-taxes-and-employer-liability |
| Annual return deadline: 31 January | "by 31 January for the preceding year" | DLA Piper (above) |
| Penalties for under-deduction / non-remittance | 40% for failure to deduct; 10% plus interest for failure to remit | DLA Piper (above) |
| PenCom recovery drives | "N36.6 billion" in unremitted contributions recovered | https://leadership.ng/pencom-recovers-n36-6bn-unremitted-contributions-as-compliance-rises-by-28/ |
| PaidHR pitches readiness | "Fully updated for Nigeria’s new tax law, effective 1 January 2026 — before your first affected payroll run." | https://www.paidhr.com/ |

INFERRED: a company found in a PenCom or state IRS enforcement action, or publicly asking about NTA payroll changes (LinkedIn posts by its HR or finance staff), is in market.
