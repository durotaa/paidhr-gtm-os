## Observed patterns (INFERRED)

- Two audiences on every page: "For Employers" / "For Employees". Benefit pairs: control and compliance for the business, money access for staff.
- Plain, conversational, slightly playful lines ("Gets You. Literally.", "Cha-ching!" in the EWA email name in a testimonial). Exclamation marks used in older product pages, fewer on the new homepage.
- Newer homepage copy moves upmarket: "African enterprises", "enterprise scale", ERP integration, "Lead with the numbers."
- Heavy use of proof numbers (₦ processed, zero defaults, ISO 27001).
- Nigerian context words: "naira", PAYE, NHF, NSITF, "Lekki".
- Capitalised "GET" (GETs, #GETPaid) as a brand device.
