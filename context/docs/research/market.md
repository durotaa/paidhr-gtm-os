# PaidHR: Market and Regulation

Raw research, not conclusions. All sources checked 2026-11-09.
Legal summaries come from law firms and advisers, not the statute text. Check the Acts or NRS guidance before quoting figures in copy.

## Nigeria tax reform (in force 1 January 2026)

**Laws.** Nigeria Tax Act 2025 (NTA) and Nigeria Tax Administration Act 2025, among others, "applied as at 1 January 2026". Signed June 2025. Sources: https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/nigeria/insights/2026/FAQs-on-payroll-taxes-and-employer-liability (2026-08-28), https://www.paidhr.com/blog/new-tax-law-in-nigeria/ (2025-11-13)

**FIRS becomes the Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS).** Source: https://www.paidhr.com/blog/new-tax-law-in-nigeria/ ; search summary of the NRS (Establishment) Act.

**PAYE bands (annual chargeable income).** Source: DLA Piper (above)

| Band | Rate |
|---|---|
| First ₦800,000 | 0% |
| Next ₦2,200,000 | 15% |
| Next ₦9,000,000 | 18% |
| Next ₦13,000,000 | 21% |
| Next ₦25,000,000 | 23% |
| Above ₦50,000,000 | 25% |

**Other payroll changes (DLA Piper, above):**
- Rent relief replaces the Consolidated Relief Allowance: 20% of annual rent, "subject to a maximum relief of ₦500,000 per year", needs proof of rent.
- NHF deductions voluntary for private-sector employees. Employers should stop them unless the employee consents. (Other sources disagree; see below.)
- Severance tax-free threshold raised from ₦10M to ₦50M. Gratuity taxable. Bonuses and 13th-month pay in the PAYE base. Benefits-in-kind valued at up to 5% of asset cost. Foreign-currency pay converted at CBN official rate on the payment date.
- Minimum-wage earners (₦70,000/month) exempt.

**Employer penalties (DLA Piper, above):**
- Late or failed PAYE filing: ₦100,000 first month, ₦50,000 each later month.
- Failure to deduct: 40% administrative penalty on the amount not deducted.
- Failure to remit: "Failure to remit attracts a 10% penalty on the unpaid amount," plus interest at the CBN rate.
- "the tax authority can pursue the employer or manager for arrears."
- Annual employee return due "by 31 January for the preceding year" (joint and several with the employer).

**Digital enforcement.** "All major filings and remittances will be digital-first"; NRS "will have better access to banking, payroll, and transaction data". Source: https://www.paidhr.com/blog/new-tax-law-in-nigeria/
- Monthly PAYE returns must be filed electronically; employers must keep accurate employee TINs (search summary of adviser commentary, not verified against statute). Source: https://gpa.net/blogs/africa/nigeria-closing-the-compliance-gap-under-new-tax-regime
- "payroll systems using the old bands are producing incorrect deductions" (adviser commentary, via search). Source: https://gpa.net/blogs/africa/nigeria-higher-paye-penalties-prompting-tighter-payroll-compliance

**Company tax (PaidHR blog summary):** 0% CIT now extends to companies under ₦100M turnover (was ₦25M); 30% above. 4% Development Levy replaces several levies. Withholding Tax Regulations in effect January 2025. Source: https://www.paidhr.com/blog/new-tax-law-in-nigeria/

INFERRED urgency: any company still on spreadsheets, old payroll software or an unpatched local tool faces personal liability for managers and a 40% penalty on under-deduction. The first annual returns under the new law fall due 31 January 2027.

## Pensions (PenCom)

- PenCom recovered "N36.6 billion" in unremitted contributions; compliance rose 28%. Sources: https://leadership.ng/pencom-recovers-n36-6bn-unremitted-contributions-as-compliance-rises-by-28/ , https://gazettengr.com/pencom-cuts-pension-approvals-to-48-hours-recovers-n36-billion-arrears/ (article dates not confirmed; Gazette page dated around 2026-08)
- NLC threatened a "crackdown on employers failing to remit workers pensions from June 1" (year not confirmed). Source: https://www.arise.tv/nlc-threatens-crackdown-on-employers-failing-to-remit-workers-pensions-from-june-1/
- PenCom plans to make Pension Protection Fund contributions mandatory for workers. Source: https://nairametrics.com/2026/04/14/pencom-to-make-pension-protection-fund-contributions-mandatory-for-workers/ (2026-04-14)
- Organised Private Sector urged FG/PenCom to suspend a proposed increase in pension contributions. Source: https://gazettengr.com/organised-sector-urges-fg-pencom-to-suspend-proposed-pension-hike/ (around 2026-07-23)
- "N99bn uncredited pension backlog highlights payroll failures". Source: https://gpa.net/blogs/africa/nigeria-n99bn-uncredited-pension-backlog-highlights-payroll-failures
- Standard rates often cited: 10% employer, 8% employee (compliance guides, not PenCom text). Source: https://www.hivedesk.com/compliance/nigeria

## NHF

- Conflicting: DLA Piper says voluntary for private-sector employees under the new law; other guides say employers must deduct 2.5% of basic and remit to FMBN. **Unresolved.** Sources: DLA Piper (above), https://www.hivedesk.com/compliance/nigeria

## Minimum wage

- ₦70,000/month under National Minimum Wage (Amendment) Act 2024, unchanged in 2026. March 2026: JNPSNC asked for ₦154,000. Lagos reportedly pays ₦85,000. Source: https://employsome.com/?p=7177 (via search)
- INFERRED: a minimum wage review would force payroll reconfiguration across many employers at once.
