## Company sizes

- Pricing tiers start at "20 - 100 Employees" (Standard) and "100+ Employees" (Enterprise). Source: https://www.paidhr.com/pricing/
- Homepage now says "built for African enterprises" and "Built for enterprise scale." Source: https://www.paidhr.com/
- CEO in 2025 framed the market as SMEs: "most small businesses on the continent don’t have access to affordable HR tools that can scale with them." Source: https://www.techarena.co.ke/2025/06/27/paidhr-workforce-efficiency-financial-wellness-smes-africa
- Average customer size, INFERRED from the homepage's own numbers: 25,000+ employees / 400+ businesses ≈ 60 employees per business. A rough figure from claims that conflict with each other.
- 2024 payroll: "Average monthly earning per employee: N240K"; "60% earn less than N150k". Source: https://report.paidhr.com/yir-2024 (same numbers appear in the 2023 report, see note below)
