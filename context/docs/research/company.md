## Pricing model

- No prices published. Tiers by headcount (exact labels):
  - "Standard" "20 - 100 Employees": HRIS, Payroll management, Statutory remittance, Earned Wage Access, Employee financial benefits, Approval Workflow, Employee Wallet, Employee Self Service, Surveys, Document Management, Disciplinary/Violation Management, Request Management, Reports
  - "Enterprise" "100+ Employees": "Everything in Standard + Dedicated support"
  - "Custom": "PaidHR suite and more" (Contact Sales)
  - Pitch: "Our flexible pricing allows you to only pay for what you need."
  Source: https://www.paidhr.com/pricing/
- EWA is "automatically included in your PaidHR plan" at "Zero cost to your company"; employees pay "a transaction fee when employees request withdrawals". Sources: https://www.paidhr.com/earned-wage-access-pade/employers/ , https://www.paidhr.com/platform/employee-benefits/
- Revenue model per TechCabal (via search snippet, page itself returned 403): employer subscriptions plus margins on airtime and bill payments through the employee wallet. Source: https://techcabal.com/2025/05/17/day-1-1000-paidhr/ (not read directly)
- Cross-border was about 15% of revenue (search summary of funding coverage, original article not confirmed). Source: search results for TechCabal/Techpoint funding coverage, 2025-06. Treat as unverified.
- Price per employee: **Not found.**

## History

- Founded: 2020 (Accion, Launch Base) vs "Founded in 2021" (PaidHR About page). Condia: research started 2020, launched July 2021.
- Previously "Pade HCM" / "PadeHCM". Renamed to PaidHR in 2024. Source: https://www.raenest.com/fr/blog/behind-the-growth-how-paidhr-found-the-right-problem-to-solve
- Started as an internal comms/engagement tool, then broader HR, then "Refocused entirely on payroll" in 2021. CEO: "The deeper pain was never the chat features or the workflow. It was the money." Source: Raenest (above)
- EWA launched 2023. Source: https://www.paidhr.com/earned-wage-access-pade/employers/
- Cross-border payroll launched Q4 2024 (12 currencies processed at launch per report). Source: https://report.paidhr.com/yir-2024
