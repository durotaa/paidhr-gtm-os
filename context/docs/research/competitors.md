# PaidHR: Competitors

Raw research, not conclusions. All sources checked 2026-11-09.
Quotes are exact. `INFERRED` marks my interpretation.

## Africa-focused HR and payroll software

### SeamlessHR (Lagos, Nigeria)
- Headline: "AI-powered HR Management Platform." Tagline: "All-In-One HR Software For Africa's Leading Companies". Sub: "Streamline HR with AI-powered software built for office-based and shift-based teams". Source: https://seamlesshr.com (fetched 2026-11-09)
- Modules: HRMS, Performance, Time Management, Payroll, Recruitment, Employee Benefits; shift-based: Attendance & Time, Mobile Self-Service, Communications & Announcements. AI assistant "Samira". Source: https://seamlesshr.com
- Countries in selector: Kenya, Uganda, Ghana, Nigeria, South Africa. "Payroll financing" in Nigeria and Kenya. Source: https://seamlesshr.com
- Founded 2017 (CB Insights via search). $10M Series A led by TLcom Capital, January 2022 (TechCabal via search; not read directly). Source: https://www.cbinsights.com/company/seamlesshr/alternatives-competitors
- Pricing: not shown on homepage.
- Publishes 2026 "top HR software in Nigeria" lists and Nigeria Tax Act payroll guides. Sources: https://seamlesshr.com/blog/top-5-hr-software-in-nigeria-in-2026/ , https://seamlesshr.com/blog/nigerias-2025-tax-reforms-key-payroll-changes-for-2026/
- INFERRED: the most direct Nigerian competitor; moving upmarket ("Africa's Leading Companies") and into shift-based workforces, overlapping PaidHR's Time & Attendance push.

### Workpay (Kenya)
- Headline: "The EOR and Payroll Solution for Africa". Sub: "Payroll, EOR, and Outsourced Payroll Solutions for growing companies in Africa". Tagline: "Simple. Compliant. Scalable." Source: https://www.myworkpay.com (fetched 2026-11-09)
- Products: Employer of Record, Outsourced Payroll, HR and Payroll Software, Financial Services, Time and Attendance, Perks and Benefits, Expense Claims, Performance Management ("Coming soon"). Source: https://www.myworkpay.com
- Claims "54 Countries", "1,000+ Companies" (another section says "over 400 companies"), "ISO 27001 Certified". Source: https://www.myworkpay.com
- Pricing $4 to $400/month per a review site (unverified). Source: https://www.linktly.com/sales-software/workpay-review/
- INFERRED: leads with EOR and pan-African reach; overlaps PaidHR Cross-Border.

### Xceed365HR (Nigeria)
- Targets organisations with 500+ employees; platform launched 2015; used by "some of Nigeria's largest financial institutions and corporations". CEO Chuma Chukwujama: "Global providers have largely treated Africa as an afterthought". Source: https://businessday.ng/news/article/african-hr-software-gap-drives-our-expansion-push-ceo-says/ (2026-05-06)
- INFERRED: enterprise competitor above PaidHR's core size band.

### Bento Africa (Nigeria): distressed
- Temporarily shut down in February 2025 after CEO Ebun Okubanjo resigned amid allegations of unremitted taxes and pensions. Board: "We will proceed to temporarily shut down operations to bring stability back to the company." Lagos State Internal Revenue Service reportedly investigated. Sources: https://techcabal.com/2025/02/10/bento-africa-temporarily-shut-down/ (via search; not read directly), https://gpa.net/blogs/africa/nigeria-bento-africa-temporarily-halts-operations
- Pitched itself as the "Deel for Africa"; reportedly had "over 1,000 businesses as clients" at peak. Source: https://weetracker.com/2025/12/04/2025-african-startup-review-unpacking-key-trends-and-events-startup-shutdowns/
- Current status: unclear (temporary pause vs closure). **Not confirmed.**
- INFERRED: Bento's former customers are a switching pool, and the episode makes remittance trust ("Zero statutory defaults") a live buying criterion.

## Global players selling into Africa

### Deel
- EOR in Nigeria and "Global Payroll" for companies with a local entity. Management fee "starting at $599 /month" on one localised page; estimated employer cost 14.5% of salary. Source: https://www.deel.com/employees/nigeria (via search, localised pages)
- INFERRED: competes with PaidHR Cross-Border for foreign companies hiring in Nigeria, not for local payroll.

### Remote, Gloroots, other EORs
- Listed as EOR options for hiring in Nigeria without a local entity. Source: https://www.gloroots.com/blog/best-eor-in-nigeria

### Workday, Gusto
- Named in PaidHR funding coverage as global platforms that "often fall short for regional needs" (paraphrase by Launch Base). Source: https://launchbaseafrica.com/2025/06/24/nigerias-paidhr-raises-1-8m-to-modernize-payroll-and-people-management-across-africa/

### Zoho People, SAP SuccessFactors
- Appear in Nigerian "top HR software" lists. Source: https://seamlesshr.com/blog/top-5-hr-software-in-nigeria-in-2026/

## Non-software alternatives (INFERRED)
- Spreadsheets plus bank bulk-upload files. PaidHR copy targets this directly: "Your days of back and forth emailing of spreadsheets and bank files are over!" Source: https://www.paidhr.com/platform/payroll/
- Outsourced payroll by accounting firms/consultants. PaidHR's whitelabel offer ("Launch your own Payroll business") targets these firms as partners rather than competitors. Source: https://www.paidhr.com/pricing/

## How PaidHR differs, by its own claims
- Embedded employee finance (EWA, Loan Marketplace, Wallet). SeamlessHR offers "payroll financing"; Workpay lists "Financial Services". INFERRED: finance is not unique, but PaidHR leads with it more.
- Compliance track record: "Zero statutory defaults or delays". Source: https://www.paidhr.com/
