# PaidHR Brand: Tone and Key Phrases

Built only from `context/docs/research/voice.md` (checked 2026-11-09). Phrases quoted exactly. Lines containing em dashes are left out (CLAUDE.md rule 3).

## Tone of voice

| Trait | Evidence (PaidHR copy) |
|---|---|
| Plain and direct | "Payroll that never misses payday"; "Compliance filed on time, automatically." |
| Two-sided: business and people | "Control for the business. Wellbeing for the people."; "For Employers" / "For Employees" on product pages |
| Warm, a little playful | "Multinational Payroll That Gets You. Literally."; "Hire right, right now." |
| Proof-led | ₦ processed, "Zero statutory defaults or delays", "ISO 27001 certified and NDPA registered" |
| Nigerian context | "without spending a naira"; PAYE, NHF, NSITF named outright |
| Getting more enterprise | "Built for enterprise scale. Proven where it counts."; "Lead with the numbers." |
| Fewer exclamation marks on newer pages (INFERRED) | Older: "Run Payroll on time, even on weekends!"; newer homepage lines mostly end in full stops |

## Brand device

- Capitalised "GET": "With PaidHR, everything GETs fulfilled", "#GETPaid".
