## Positioning statement

For Nigerian businesses with 20 or more staff who still run payroll and statutory remittances by hand or across disconnected tools, PaidHR is the HR and payroll platform that pays people on time, files PAYE, pension, NHF and NSITF automatically, and gives employees earned wage access at no cost to the employer. Unlike spreadsheets or general HR suites, PaidHR is already updated for the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 and processed ₦934M in PAYE and ₦754M in pension in 2025 with zero defaults.
