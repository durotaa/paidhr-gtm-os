## Where PaidHR wins (INFERRED from research)

- Remittance trust: published 2025 PAYE and pension totals, 100% on time, "Zero statutory defaults" [co]. Matters more after Bento [cp].
- Employee finance as a free benefit: EWA at "Zero cost to your company", PaidHR fronts the capital [co].
- Nigerian payroll detail: multi-state remittance, NTA 2025 updates in place before 1 Jan 2026 [co].
- Size fit: tiers start at 20 employees [co], below Xceed365HR's 500+ focus [cp].

## Where PaidHR loses (INFERRED)

- Legal EOR needed: no PaidHR EOR product found; Workpay and Deel sell it [cp].
- Pan-African local compliance: Workpay claims 54 countries; PaidHR's statutory evidence is Nigeria only [cp], [co].
- Buyers wanting AI-led HR or a long track record: SeamlessHR leads with AI and is older and better funded [cp].
- Price-only buyers: free tools exist (LeadstackHR) [cp].

## How to respond

| They say | Respond with | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "We use SeamlessHR / Workpay" | Ask how remittances are confirmed; offer EWA at no employer cost as the switch reason | [co] Pricing, Modules |
| "We need EOR" | No EOR product found. If they only need to pay people abroad, offer Cross-Border (CEO: "We enable companies to pay their employees across countries without needing to set up a local office,"). Open question: does that cover employment compliance abroad? | [cp], [sg] Expansion |
| "Payroll startups are risky" (Bento) | 2025 remittance totals, ISO 27001, NDPA, Accion Venture Lab backing. Do not name Bento in copy | [co], [cp] |
| "Spreadsheets work fine" | NTA 2025 penalties: 40% for failure to deduct, 10% plus interest for failure to remit (verify against statute before use) | [mk] |
