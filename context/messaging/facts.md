**Formatting rules for proof**
- Write "PaidHR" and "Famasi" exactly.
- Naira as ₦ with "M" or "bn". No em dashes.
- Time-bound numbers (2026 YTD) carry their date and are rechecked before each campaign.

## Numbers to keep fuzzy (never quote as hard numbers)

- Customer counts (570+, 400+, 250+, 100+) and employee counts (43,000+, 25,000+, 20,000+, 12k+): conflict across sources.
- Currency and country counts (45+, 49, 100+, 120, 150+): conflict.
- Team size, total funding ($2.3M vs ~$2.9M), founding year (2020 vs 2021).
- 2023 and 2024 Year in Review figures: reused data.
- "Zero churn": unverified.
- NTA penalty percentages: law-firm commentary, not statute.

## Banned

### Banned angles
- Naming or mocking failed or rival providers (Bento, SeamlessHR, Workpay).
- Guarantees ("never miss", "zero defaults guaranteed"). Past record only.
- Penalty scare figures until checked against the statute.
- Diagnosing the prospect's team as failing.

### Banned words and phrasing (company-specific)
Global list: `knowledge-base/playbooks/copy-bans.yaml`. Plus:
- "Pade", "PadeHCM", "Pade HCM" (old name)
- "Series B" (founder quote, internal brag)
- Em dashes

### Never in copy
- Employee testimonials naming employers without approval (Rise, Rollpay)
- Anonymous source "Eze"
