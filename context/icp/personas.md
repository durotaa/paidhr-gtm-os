## 1. Champion: HR / People lead

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

**Top three pains, own words** [cu]
1. Slow request handling: "the turn around in addressing leave requests, loans, salary advances and expense reimbursement" (Nikky Taurus, after: "has been very impressive")
2. Tools that cover only payroll: "We only needed payroll but Pade has helped us become more effective in managing employee information, setting OKRs and managing team activities." (Kredete)
3. Hard-to-learn software: "easy to navigate, even for starters, and not difficult to figure out" (Jamit, as the gain)
